Mac App Store Privacy Policy

Audio stays on the Mac.

No TalkType servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Below: how audio, transcripts, permissions and optional keys behave on macOS. Last updated 5 November 2026.

At a glance

100% offline default Apple's on-device speech recognizer runs locally on the Mac's Neural Engine without uploading voice audio.
Zero telemetry No analytics SDKs, no crash uploaders, no advertising identifiers, and no TalkType backend server.
Keychain isolation Optional personal API keys stay off by default and live solely in the macOS Keychain.
Local History & clipboard Transcripts copy to the local clipboard (⌘V) and keep up to 50 takes inside the sandboxed app container.

Default on-device engine

Out of the box, TalkType transcribes speech locally on the Mac's Neural Engine through Apple's Speech framework configured for on-device recognition. Voice audio stays on the Mac, nothing reaches this website, and no TalkType server exists to receive audio or text.

Permissions used

TalkType requests Microphone access (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone) and Speech Recognition access (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition). Audio capture runs strictly during an active take started via ⌃⌥ Space or by clicking the ghost in the menu bar popover, and stops the instant the shortcut releases, the ghost is clicked again, or Escape cancels the take.

Optional cloud engines (strictly off by default)

Two optional personal-key cloud integrations stay completely disabled unless an API key is added from the menu bar settings:

  • Deepgram Nova-3 — streams audio directly from the Mac over encrypted TLS to Deepgram for real-time recognition and optional conversation speaker labels, with model training turned off on every session.
  • Google Gemini — sends finished transcripts directly from the Mac over encrypted TLS to Google for optional prose polish and custom vocabulary formatting.

Those requests travel straight between the Mac and the chosen provider under that provider's own terms and privacy policy. TalkType never relays, logs, or sees the traffic. Removing a key returns TalkType strictly to offline on-device recognition.

Keychain storage

Optional API keys are stored solely inside the macOS Keychain as items scoped to TalkType. Keys are never written to plain-text preferences, never backed up to external servers by TalkType, and never sent anywhere other than the matching provider's authentication header.

Clipboard, local History, and preferences

Completed transcripts copy locally to the macOS clipboard ready for ⌘V in any app. Up to 50 recent takes, custom vocabulary entries, language selection (English / Español), and HUD layout preferences remain inside TalkType's sandboxed container on the Mac. Exporting History writes a plain .txt file to a chosen local folder, and clearing History erases stored takes immediately.

App Store privacy label and contact

On the Mac App Store, TalkType declares Data Not Collected: no analytics, no identifiers, no diagnostics, and no tracking across apps or websites. Mac App Store billing and receipts stay with Apple. Support messages sent to pibulus@gmail.com stay strictly within that email conversation for resolving the note.
