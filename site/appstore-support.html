Everyday mechanics

Hold ⌃⌥ Space anywhere Holding Control + Option + Space starts listening, and releasing finishes the take. A quick tap on ⌃⌥ Space latches hands-free mode.
Popover ghost & Escape Clicking the ghost inside the menu bar popover starts a take, and a second click finishes it. Pressing Escape while listening cancels cleanly.
Clipboard ready for ⌘V Finished transcripts copy straight to the macOS clipboard so ⌘V pastes clean text into any open document, editor, or chat box.
50 takes of local History Recent transcripts stay inside the History tab up to 50 takes, exportable to plain .txt and clearable at any time.

Permissions on macOS

TalkType relies on two standard macOS permissions and nothing else:

  • Microphone (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone) so the active audio input can hear speech during a take.
  • Speech Recognition (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Speech Recognition) so Apple's on-device recognizer can transcribe offline on the Mac's Neural Engine.

When either permission was declined on first launch, toggling TalkType back on in Privacy & Security and reopening the app restores dictation immediately. Right-clicking the menu bar ghost surfaces any missing permission at the top of the menu.

Menu bar controls

Right-clicking (or Control-clicking) the ghost in the macOS menu bar opens the complete control menu:

  • Voice Mode — chooses Apple's offline Neural Engine recognizer or optional cloud recognition once an API key is stored.
  • Conversations — enables multi-speaker labels during cloud transcription takes.
  • Auto-Polish — runs an optional prose cleanup pass after transcription when a Gemini key is configured.
  • Custom Vocabulary — teaches preferred spellings for names, places, studios, and technical terms.
  • Language — switches recognition between English and Español.
  • Left/Right Click swap — flips whether a primary click opens the popover or toggles dictation directly.
  • HUD Position — places the floating waveform capsule where it fits the display best.
  • Launch at Login — keeps the ghost ready in the menu bar after restarting macOS.
  • Setup Guide, Privacy Policy, and Quit.

Menu bar icon hidden behind the notch

macOS hides status bar icons when the menu bar runs out of horizontal room, especially on MacBook displays with a camera housing. Closing a few neighbouring menu bar items frees space so the ghost reappears; ⌃⌥ Space continues to work even while the icon is crowded out.

Engines and privacy

Apple's speech recognizer runs 100% offline on the Mac's Neural Engine by default — audio stays on the Mac, with zero TalkType servers, zero user accounts, zero analytics, and zero tracking. Optional personal-key cloud engines — Deepgram Nova-3 streaming to api.deepgram.com (with mip_opt_out=true model-training opt-out) and Google Gemini prose polish to generativelanguage.googleapis.com — remain strictly off by default until an API key is saved into the macOS Keychain (kSecClassGenericPassword). Full details live on the Privacy Policy.
