Privacy

Audio stays on the Mac.

TalkType runs with no backend, no account system, and no analytics. Below: what stays on the Mac, what can leave it, and which third party receives it. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Default engine

Apple's speech recognizer runs locally on the Mac's Neural Engine. Voice audio is not uploaded to Apple, and it does not reach this website either — there is no TalkType server to reach.

Optional cloud engines

Adding a personal Deepgram or Google Gemini API key turns on streaming cloud recognition, with model training turned off or an optional prose polish pass. When those engines run, audio or finished text travels directly from the Mac to that provider over TLS using the stored key. Deepgram and Google process requests under their own terms; TalkType never sees the traffic.

Keychain storage

API keys live inside the macOS Keychain, scoped to TalkType. Keys are not written to plain preference files, not synced by TalkType, and never sent anywhere other than the chosen provider's authentication header.

History and settings

Up to 50 recent transcripts, custom vocabulary entries, and menu bar preferences stay locally on the Mac. History exports to plain .txt locally and clears immediately from the History tab. Nothing syncs to external servers.

Data collected by this website

Direct purchases open Square's hosted checkout. Square collects the email address and card details needed for the payment receipt; card data never touches this site. This website sets no analytics cookies and loads no third-party trackers. Standard host logs record basic request metadata such as IP address and browser user agent.

Purchases and support

Notes sent to pibulus@gmail.com stay in that inbox only as long as the thread requires. Direct purchase receipts stay with Square.

Mac App Store edition

The Mac App Store edition declares Data Not Collected: no analytics, no identifiers, no advertising, and no tracking. Its standalone policy lives at mac.talktype.app/appstore-privacy.

Contact

Direct questions reach pibulus@gmail.com. The companion web-app policy lives at talktype.app/privacy.
