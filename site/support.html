Support

One person answers.

One developer builds and supports TalkType in Melbourne. Notes to pibulus@gmail.com with the macOS version and what happened usually receive a reply within one day, Melbourne time.

Menu bar ghost hidden behind the notch

macOS hides extra menu bar items when horizontal space fills up, especially on a MacBook with a camera housing. Closing a few other menu bar items or using a bar organizer such as Ice or Bartender brings the ghost back into view. Dictation continues running either way.

Nothing pastes automatically at the cursor

Automatic cursor paste relies on Accessibility under System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → TalkType. Without Accessibility enabled, finished transcripts still copy straight to the clipboard, and ⌘V pastes them anywhere. Password and secure-input fields block synthetic keystrokes across macOS by design.

Microphone or Speech Recognition permission

Microphone and Speech Recognition toggles live under System Settings → Privacy & Security. Enabling TalkType there and reopening the app restores audio capture immediately. Right-clicking the menu bar ghost highlights any permission still missing.

Shortcut keys and cancelling

Either ⌥ Option triggers push-to-talk, and a quick tap latches hands-free mode for longer takes. When another tool claims a modifier or a compact keyboard maps keys differently, right-clicking the menu bar ghost offers Either ⌥ Option, Right ⌥ Option, Left ⌥ Option, Globe / fn, Right ⌘, or ⌃⌥ Space. Pressing Escape while listening cancels the take cleanly without pasting.

Redownloading the DMG

The current notarized build stays at talktype.app/downloads/TalkType.dmg. No licence key or receipt lookup gates the download.

Refunds

Forwarding the Square receipt to pibulus@gmail.com within 30 days triggers a full refund, no interrogation. A short line on what missed the mark helps improve the next build.

Privacy

Audio stays on the Mac by default, optional keys live in the macOS Keychain, and zero analytics or account servers exist. Full details live on the privacy page.
